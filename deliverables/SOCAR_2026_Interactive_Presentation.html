Before fitting any model, inspecting how fluids physically travel from the wellhead choke to the surface network uncovered a hidden operational regime shift.
Physical Flow Narrative — From Choke Hydraulics to Causal Modeling
01 · TEST SEPARATOR vs. PRODUCTION MANIFOLD ROUTING

During periodic well tests, a single well is isolated into a lower-pressure Test Separator (e.g., GL-01 downstream pressure P_ds ≈ 169 psia). On all remaining production days, wells commingle into the higher-pressure Production Manifold (P_ds ≈ 197 psia). Thus every well experiences a systematic +25 to +35 psi backpressure shift between test days and normal production.

02 · WHY NAIVE ML VIOLATES CHOKE PHYSICS

By orifice physics, higher downstream backpressure reduces ΔP_choke = WHP − P_ds and can only restrict (or leave unchanged, in critical flow) the liquid rate. Yet across the 10 wells, higher-rate wells happen to flow against higher separator pressures, creating a spurious cross-well correlation (r = +0.65). Naive ML mistakes +28 psi higher manifold backpressure for massive extra production — hallucinating 1,875 STB/d (+104% phantom oil) on GL-01!

03 · CAUSAL PHYSICS RESOLUTION

First, we cleanse sensor spikes (-999.25 sentinels, Hampel filter) and harmonize gas unit anomalies. Second, we replace raw P_ds with the causal choke driving force ΔP_choke and critical/subcritical choke flow capacity. Third, we sweep physics-gated synthetic points across both separator and manifold pressure bounds so the ensemble learns true monotonic hydraulics (919.6 STB/d).
