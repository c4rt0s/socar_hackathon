Key empirical findings:
1. **Diameter vs. Area choke mapping:** `choke_opening_pct` is **linear in bean diameter $d$** (so area $A \propto d^2$). $d^2 \sqrt{\Delta P_{\text{choke}}}$ achieves $r = +0.76$ to $+0.99$ on 9 of 10 wells, whereas linear-area $d^1 \sqrt{\Delta P}$ is negative on 6 of 10 wells.
2. **Why `GL-02` has $r(q_{\text{liq}}, d^2\sqrt{\Delta P}) \approx 0$:** Across `GL-02`'s 8 tests, `d^2 * sqrt(dp)` barely varies (`8.95` to `9.36`, only 4.5% span, because choke opened `51.6% → 60.9%` while `whp` fell `231 → 195 psia`), while `qliq` drifts `2869 → 2718` (~5%) with ~3% test noise and rising WC/GLR; including upstream density via $d^2 \sqrt{P_{wh}\Delta P}$ gives $r = +0.609$.
3. **Depletion vs. Drawdown in $P_{dh}$:** On wells with wide choke adjustments (`GL-03, 04, 05, 07, 08, 09`), $r(q_{\text{liq}}, P_{dh}) = -0.77$ to $-0.97$ (classic IPR slope). On `GL-01` (`+0.518`) and `GL-02` (`+0.609`), $P_r(t)$ depletion causes $P_{dh}$ and $q_{\text{liq}}$ to decline together over Jan–Sep, masking static $P_{dh}$ correlation until $P_r(t)$ is modeled.
4. **Tubing $\Delta P$ ambiguity:** $r(q_{\text{liq}}, \Delta P_{\text{tub}})$ flips sign across wells (`-0.98` on `GL-05` vs `+0.96` on `GL-10`) because tubing $\Delta P$ is the sum of **friction** (increases with $q$) and **hydrostatic head** (increases with WC and decreases with GLR), and changing choke alters $P_{wh}$ (gas expansion) and $q$ simultaneously. Thus VLP inversion requires full mechanistic hydrostatic + GLR + WC + friction physics, not raw $\Delta P_{\text{tub}}$ regression.

- **Note on downhole valve $\Delta P$ estimation:** Linearly interpolating tubing pressure between `whp` and `dh_pressure` yields an apparent negative $\Delta P_{\text{valve}}$ because tubing below `gl_valve_tvd_ft` has no lift gas (higher hydrostatic gradient `~0.35–0.40 psi/ft`), whereas tubing above `gl_valve_tvd_ft` is lightened by lift gas. VLP must segment tubing at `gl_valve_tvd_ft`.
