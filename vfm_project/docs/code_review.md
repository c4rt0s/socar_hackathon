# Code Review & Audit of `vfm_project/`

**Scope:** Line-by-line review of all modules in [src/vfm/](file:///C:/Users/UFAZ/Downloads/Media-20261121T063614Z-1-001/Media/vfm_project/src/vfm) and scripts [01](file:///C:/Users/UFAZ/Downloads/Media-20261121T063614Z-1-001/Media/vfm_project/scripts/01_audit_and_prepare.py)–[04](file:///C:/Users/UFAZ/Downloads/Media-20261121T063614Z-1-001/Media/vfm_project/scripts/04_predict_daily_rates.py) for data leakage, unit mismatches, physical errors, numerical/missing-data edge cases, and allocation defects.

**Executive Summary:** The existing pipeline successfully fixes raw `well_tests.csv` sign flips (`5` rows) and `GL-06` Sm3/d gas logging (`÷28.3168466`) and removes `-999.25` sentinels/duplicates from `high_freq_data.csv`. However, it exhibits **critical physical, statistical, and accounting flaws** that motivate replacing `HybridVFM` with a calibrated mechanistic physics VFM (`PhysicsVFM`) and fixing state/allocation logic:
1. **Severe model overfitting & wrong target physics (`hybrid_model.py`, `hydraulics.py`):** Pools 85 tests across 26 collinear features (with `numpy_ridge` fallback because `scikit-learn`/`lightgbm` are not installed), lacks per-well $J$, $P_r(t)$, $C_d$, and VLP calibration, omits `datum_tvd_ft` and critical choke clamping, ignores `choke_max_size_64ths`, and predicts slow waterflood `WC` and undersaturated `GOR` from instantaneous pressures/temperatures.
2. **False shut-in trigger on `dp_choke <= 2.0 psi` (`states.py`):** Incorrectly classifies 6 open-choke minutes on `GL-03` (`2025-09-06 08:20–08:26` at `50.0%` choke) as `is_shut_in=True` while leaving adjacent low-`dp` minutes (`08:25`, `08:27`) as `is_steady_flowing`.
3. **Midnight test-anchoring bug & missing calendar day (`reconciler.py`):** Anchors tests on `test_start.floor("D")` even though **59/85 tests span midnight** (some with only 9–35 min on `test_start` day), omits `total_gas_rate_alloc_mscfd`, drops `GL-06` on `2025-06-24` (`272/273` days) due to a 36h SCADA gap, and computes `uptime_frac` over observed minutes instead of `1440`.
4. **Latent leakage & time-gap interpolation bugs (`cleaning.py`, `states.py`, `hydraulics.py`, `validation.py`):** Centered Hampel filter peeks 7 min ahead; `bfill()` on `choke_opening_pct` leaks future choke if data precedes first event; index-based `.interpolate(limit=5)` and `.rolling(15/60)` bridge across 24–36h SCADA gaps; `chronological_holdout_eval` splits on `test_start < cutoff` (splitting tests crossing midnight at cutoff).

## Module-by-Module Findings

### 1. [src/vfm/config.py](file:///C:/Users/UFAZ/Downloads/Media-20261121T063614Z-1-001/Media/vfm_project/src/vfm/config.py)
- **[Low] Hardcoded `PROJECT_ROOT` relative to package path (`L11`, `L24`):** `PROJECT_ROOT = Path(__file__).resolve().parents[2]` assumes editable/source execution. If `vfm` is installed non-editably into `site-packages` (via `pyproject.toml`), `load_config()` fails to locate `configs/default.json`, and `resolve_path()` resolves relative to `site-packages` even when an explicit `config_path` is passed. *Fix:* Anchor `project_root` from `config_path.parent.parent` when `config_path` is supplied.

### 2. [src/vfm/io/loaders.py](file:///C:/Users/UFAZ/Downloads/Media-20261121T063614Z-1-001/Media/vfm_project/src/vfm/io/loaders.py)
- **[Medium] Incomplete sign-check coverage & no post-fix balance assertion (`L21–28`, `L64–78`):** `SIGN_CHECK_COLS` checks the 6 rate columns (`gas_lift_rate_mscfd`, `oil_rate_stbd`, `water_rate_stbd`, `gas_rate_mscfd`, `total_gas_rate_mscfd`, `liquid_rate_stbd`) and repairs all 5 negative entries observed in `well_tests.csv`. However, it does not check `whp_psia`, `whp_downstream_psia`, `dh_pressure_psia`, `annulus_pressure_psia`, `water_cut_pct`, `gor_scf_stb`, or `glr_scf_stb` for sign flips, nor does it re-verify `oil + water ≈ liquid` and `gas + gas_lift ≈ total_gas` after sign repair.
- **[Medium] Coupled Sm3/d conversion assumption (`L79–96`):** Divides *both* `gas_rate_mscfd` and `total_gas_rate_mscfd` by `28.3168466` whenever per-well median `gas_rate / expected_form_gas > 15.0`. While this is exact for `GL-06` (post-fix `max |total_gas - (gas + gas_lift)| = 0.0036 Mscf/d`), it assumes `total_gas_rate_mscfd` always shares the unit of `gas_rate_mscfd` and operates per-well rather than verifying `(total_gas - gas_lift) ≈ gas` independently on each column and row.
- **[Low] Duplicate `choke_events` timestamps not handled (`L42–44`):** `load_static_tables` sorts by `["well_name", "timestamp"]` without deduplicating simultaneous timestamps, making downstream `merge_asof` order-dependent if duplicate choke timestamps ever occur.

### 3. [src/vfm/preprocessing/cleaning.py](file:///C:/Users/UFAZ/Downloads/Media-20261121T063614Z-1-001/Media/vfm_project/src/vfm/preprocessing/cleaning.py)
- **[High] Index-based interpolation bridges multi-hour SCADA gaps (`L80`):** `s.interpolate(method="linear", limit=interp_limit, limit_area="inside")` operates on row indices rather than timestamps. Every well in `high_freq_data.csv` has 2–5 timestamp gaps > 60s (up to `1440–2160` missing minutes, e.g. `GL-04 2025-09-21..22`, `GL-06 2025-06-23..25`, and a field-wide `270 min` gap `2025-08-09 01:09–05:40`). If a boundary sample adjacent to a 36h gap was masked as a sentinel or spike, `.interpolate(limit=5)` linearly interpolates across 36 hours as if they were 1-minute steps. *Fix:* Use time-aware interpolation (`method="time"` or enforce `dt <= 60s` gaps only).
- **[Medium] Centered Hampel filter introduces minor look-ahead (`L68–77`):** `rolling(window=15, center=True, min_periods=5)` looks 7 minutes into the future. While empirical inspection of `GL-01`, `GL-02`, `GL-04`, `GL-06`, `GL-08`, and `GL-10` shows it did not distort shut-in/restart ramps in the cached files, a centered filter is non-causal for real-time minute scoring.
- **[Low] Duplicate resolution ignores NaN/sentinel quality (`L36–40`):** `out.duplicated(subset=["timestamp"], keep="first")` unconditionally keeps the first row even if it holds `-999.25` while the duplicate holds a valid reading, and fails to include `well_name` in `subset` if a multi-well DataFrame is passed.

### 4. [src/vfm/preprocessing/states.py](file:///C:/Users/UFAZ/Downloads/Media-20261121T063614Z-1-001/Media/vfm_project/src/vfm/preprocessing/states.py)
- **[High] `dp_choke <= 2.0 psi` rule causes false shut-ins (`L43–47`):** `is_shut_in = choke_closed | (dp_choke <= 2.0)` flags **6 isolated minutes on `GL-03` (`2025-09-06 08:20–08:24` and `08:26`)** where `choke_opening_pct = 50.0%` and `dh_pressure_psia = 2511.8–2514.3 psia`, solely because manifold backpressure rose to `~193 psia` (`dp_choke = -1.17` to `+1.38 psi`). Worse, `08:25` (`dp = 2.74 psi`) and `08:27` (`dp = 2.39 psi`) are classified as `is_steady_flowing=True` with no hysteresis or stabilization buffer. Any PBU extractor relying on `is_shut_in` would ingest `GL-03` as a fake shut-in! *Fix:* Separate **true closed-in state** (`choke_opening_pct <= 0.5`) from **backpressure stall / low-$\Delta P$ transient** (`choke > 0.5` and `dp_choke <= 2.0`).
- **[Medium] Latent future leakage via `.bfill()` (`L39`):** `merged["choke_opening_pct"].bfill()` propagates future choke openings backward to any telemetry before the first choke event. It did not activate here only because all 10 wells have an initial event at `2025-01-01 00:00:00`. *Fix:* Never backfill choke events; treat pre-event rows as unknown (`NaN`) or require an explicit initial state.
- **[Medium] NaN sensor blind spot & `t0` boundary alignment (`L43–48`):** When `whp_psia` or `whp_downstream_psia` is `NaN` (e.g., `235` NaNs on `GL-02` `whp_psia`, `115` on `GL-06`, `40` on `GL-01` `whp_downstream_psia`), `dp_choke <= 2.0` evaluates to `False`. Also, at exact choke-change timestamps (`GL-01 2025-04-15 08:00`, `GL-02 2025-03-08 20:02`), the 1-min sample still holds pre-change pressures/lift-gas while `is_shut_in` has already flipped (`+1 min` sensor lag), and a fixed `30 min` buffer does not capture multi-hour wellbore storage transients after 48–72h shut-ins or gas-lift step changes.
- **[Low] Single-well assumption (`L24`):** `well = str(out["well_name"].iloc[0])` silently mis-joins choke events if a multi-well DataFrame is passed.

### 5. [src/vfm/physics/hydraulics.py](file:///C:/Users/UFAZ/Downloads/Media-20261121T063614Z-1-001/Media/vfm_project/src/vfm/physics/hydraulics.py)
- **[Critical] Incomplete choke equation & unused `choke_max_size_64ths` (`L31`, `L50–59`):**
  - `choke_max_size_64ths` is merged on `L31` and **never used**; `choke_frac = pct / 100` omits actual bean diameter $d_{\text{in}} = (\text{pct}/100)\cdot(\text{choke\_max\_size\_64ths}/64)$ and physical area $A_{\text{ft}^2}$.
  - `choke_flow_proxy = (choke_frac ** 1.89) * np.sqrt(dp_choke)` mixes empirical Gilbert exponent `1.89` with subcritical $\sqrt{\Delta P}$ **without critical-flow clamping** ($r_c \approx 0.55$) and **without upstream mixture density $\rho_{\text{mix}}$**. Empirically, `GL-04` (`98.5%` critical), `GL-10` (`86.7%` critical), and `GL-06` (`67.4%` critical) operate predominantly in critical flow, and omitting $\rho_{\text{mix}}$ nearly doubles choke MAPE (`1.76–1.85%` vs `3.01%`).
- **[Critical] Missing datum TVD correction & mechanistic IPR/VLP (`L61–69`):**
  - `datum_tvd_ft` from `reservoir_pvt.csv` is never loaded or applied, omitting a `+150` to `+420 ft` (`+54` to `+170 psi`) hydrostatic offset between gauge and reservoir datum.
  - `tubing_grad_psi_ft = dp_tubing / gauge_tvd_ft` averages over the entire wellbore, ignoring the gas-lift injection point at `gl_valve_tvd_ft`, real-gas Z-factor expansion, liquid holdup/slip, and Darcy-Weisbach friction.
- **[Medium] Silent clipping & singularity in `gl_to_choke_proxy` (`L49`, `L62`, `L70`):** Clipping `dp_choke` and `dp_tubing` at `0.0` masks sensor inversions (`GL-03` `-1.17 psi`). On shut-in or `dp_choke == 0`, `choke_flow_proxy == 0` sends `gl_to_choke_proxy = gas_lift_rate / 0.01` up to `~10^5`.
- **[Medium] Rolling features ignore timestamp gaps & shut-in transitions (`L79–82`):** `rolling(w, min_periods=3)` computes `15m`/`60m` means and stds seamlessly across 24–36h SCADA outages and across shut-in/restart steps.
- **[Medium] `well_age_years` confounds cross-well vintage with depletion (`L74–75`):** Ranges from ~2–15 years across wells vs 0.75 years in-sample, acting as a well-ID proxy in pooled ML models.

### 8. [src/vfm/modeling/hybrid_model.py](file:///C:/Users/UFAZ/Downloads/Media-20261121T063614Z-1-001/Media/vfm_project/src/vfm/modeling/hybrid_model.py)
- **[Critical] Severe overfitting & fallback to global linear Ridge (`L44–67`, `L99–147`):** Fits 26 features on 85 pooled well tests (~55–65 in chronological train split). Because `lightgbm` and `scikit-learn` are not installed in the active environment, `detect_best_backend()` falls back to `numpy_ridge`, fitting a single global linear model with zero per-well IPR/choke/VLP parameters.
- **[Critical] Unphysical instantaneous prediction of `WC` and `GOR` (`L145–146`, `L154–155`):** Predicts `water_cut_frac` and `log1p(gor_form)` from instantaneous pressures/temperatures across wells instead of slow per-well waterflood trends (`+0.44%` to `+1.07%/30d`) and $R_{sb}$-anchored undersaturated GOR (`mean GOR/Rs = 0.99`).
- **[High] Silent global-mean imputation of missing sensors (`L57`, `L66`):** `_NumpyRidge.predict` replaces `NaN`s with `0.0` in standardized space (`global training mean`). During downhole/WHP sensor outages (`235` NaNs on `GL-02` `whp_psia`, `235` on `GL-07` `dh_pressure_psia`, `155` on `GL-04` `dh_pressure_psia`), it silently outputs predictions derived from field-average pressures.
- **[High] Silent `0.0` fill on missing `gas_lift_rate_mscfd` (`L157`):** Replaces `NaN` lift gas with `0.0` (`595` NaNs on `GL-03`), under-reporting `total_gas_rate_mscfd` during meter dropouts.

### 10. [src/vfm/allocation/reconciler.py](file:///C:/Users/UFAZ/Downloads/Media-20261121T063614Z-1-001/Media/vfm_project/src/vfm/allocation/reconciler.py)
- **[Critical] Drops full SCADA-outage days & inflates `uptime_frac` (`L14–34`):**
  - Groups only over observed rows (`df.groupby(["well_name", "date"])`). Because `GL-06` has a 36h SCADA gap (`2025-06-23 21:57 -> 2025-06-25 09:58`), **`2025-06-24` is completely absent (`272/273` days)**, yielding `2729` daily rows instead of `2730`.
  - Computes `uptime_frac = flowing_minutes / n_minutes` instead of separating operational uptime from `data_completeness = n_valid_minutes / 1440`. On `GL-04 2025-09-21` (`180` observed rows), `uptime_frac` falsely reports `1.0000`. Also ignores `NaN` predictions in `mean()` while counting those rows as `flowing_minutes`.
- **[Critical] Anchors well tests on `test_start` calendar day instead of `[test_start, test_end]` (`L49–56`, `L75–83`):**
  - `wt["date"] = wt["test_start"].dt.floor("D")` aligns each 12–24h well test to its starting calendar date. Empirically, **59 of 85 tests span midnight**, including `GL-08 2025-02-20 23:51 -> 02-21 11:51` (**9 minutes** on `test_start` day!) and `GL-07 2025-04-20 23:25 -> 04-21 15:25` (**35 minutes** on `test_start` day!). Anchoring must aggregate VFM predictions over the exact `[test_start, test_end]` interval and interpolate against the test midpoint $t_{\text{mid}}$.
- **[High] Omits `total_gas_rate_alloc_mscfd` (`L58–85`):** Anchors `oil`, `water`, `gas` (formation), and recomputes `liquid_rate_alloc_stbd`, but never updates or exports `total_gas_rate_alloc_mscfd`.
- **[Medium] Manifold reconciliation defects (`L88–125`):** `reconcile_to_manifold_totals` groups by `"date"` alone (ignoring `production_manifold` A vs B), assigns `w_vec = np.maximum(q_vec, 1.0)` which can allocate positive rates to **shut-in wells (`q_vec == 0`)**, breaks Lagrange optimality via post-clipping rescaling, and forgets to update `liquid_rate_alloc_stbd`.
